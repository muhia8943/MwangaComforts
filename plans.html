Our Welfare Support Plans

Four plans, one promise of care

Our support packages are built around real family needs — healthcare, dignity in loss, education, and financial relief — so members can face life's moments with confidence.

01 — Mwanga Afya Cover

Health & Maternity Support

Mwanga Afya Cover supports members during hospitalization and maternity by helping cover inpatient and maternal care costs. It only caters for inpatient services, and claims should be made within 72 hours of discharge, and not more than 7 working days thereafter.

The Maternity program add-on provides pampers and a Ksh 5,000 gift hamper for mother and baby after child delivery, available in full to members who have been with us for 12 months.

02 — Mwanga Farewell Cover

A Dignified Send-Off

This package supports families during bereavement by covering essential funeral and burial expenses — from body preparation and transport to tents and seating — helping families give their loved ones a dignified send-off without the burden of sudden expenses.

Mwanga Comforts caters for up to three funerals per family per year, spaced four months apart unless accident-related. Claims should be reported at least 72 hours before the burial date.

03 — Mwanga Hybrid Cover

Combined Protection, One Membership

The Hybrid package brings together the Mwanga Afya Cover and the Mwanga Farewell Cover in one plan, providing broader protection under a single membership. It supports families during hospitalization and, when needed, helps cover final arrangements — offering peace of mind across life's most difficult moments.

04 — Masomo Cover

Education Support

This package is designed to help families manage education-related costs and plan for their children's learning journey. It supports school-related needs and education planning, easing the financial strain on parents and guardians.

By promoting education continuity, Masomo Cover helps children remain in school and build brighter, more stable futures.
